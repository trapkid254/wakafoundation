Our Story

The Story Behind WAKA Foundation

Born and raised in Kenya, Waka witnessed firsthand the devastating cycle of poverty — children dropping out of school, youth with no opportunities, families without healthcare. Rather than look away, he chose to act.

In 2020, he founded WAKA Will Work Foundation with a simple but powerful belief: every human being deserves dignity, opportunity, and the chance to thrive. Starting with a single community in Nairobi's Mathare Valley, the foundation has grown to serve 47+ communities across Kenya.

"Will Work means we don't wait for someone else to fix it. We show up, roll up our sleeves, and do the work — together."
— Waka, Founder
